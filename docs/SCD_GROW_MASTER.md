# SCD GROW MASTER

**Name:** SCD GROW  
**Long name:** SCD GROW — Partner & Sponsor Growth Engine  
**Tagline:** Trova. Connetti. Sviluppa. Rinnova.

## Mission
Continuously create, qualify, develop and retain sponsor/partner opportunities through a futuristic CRM and research/development engine.

## Core
Public partner experience, Partner Hub, CRM/stakeholders, lead radar, commercial pipeline, Lia Sponsor, proposals, partner microsites, LED/Sponsor Wall, conventions, Solidarity Fund, projects, suppliers, assets, proof/reporting, renewals, follow-up and research automation.

## Growth loop
RESEARCH -> VERIFY -> QUALIFY -> CONNECT -> PROPOSE -> ACTIVATE -> PROVE -> REPORT -> RENEW -> EXPAND.

## Product standard
Research-heavy, proactive, measurable, visually premium, relationship-centric and continuous-development oriented.

## Guardrail
Prospect is not sponsor. Proposal is not contract. Research is not fact until verified.

## Command
`SCDGROW:RUN` invokes `WEBAPP:MASTER`.
