# C.E.P.A. 360 MASTER

**Name:** C.E.P.A. 360  
**Long name:** C.E.P.A. 360 by Maglia Assicurazioni  
**Tagline:** Dalla storia della consulenza al futuro della protezione.

## Mission
Create a new consulting experience through C.E.P.A. while turning the long operational history, partnerships, products, collaborators and relationships of Maglia Assicurazioni into a modern agency operating system and super CRM.

## Public world
C.E.P.A., education, consulting journey, previdenza, protezione, welfare, salute, tutela, events, Center C.E.P.A., SAP network, territory and collaborations.

## Private world
Maglia 360 Control Room, customers, partner/products, collaborators, documents/contracts, Lia Workbench, AI Mail/Chat, Network Radar/IVASS, development, activities/deadlines, Recovery, relationship graph and history intelligence.

## Principle
The past is structured evidence. The product must learn from verified history to build future relationships, services and consulting opportunities.

## Product standard
Public: contemporary, educational, authoritative and human.
Private: futuristic CRM/control room, action-first, relationship-centric and deeply integrated.

## Command
`CEPA360:RUN` invokes `WEBAPP:MASTER`.
