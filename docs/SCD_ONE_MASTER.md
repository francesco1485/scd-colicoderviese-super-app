# SCD ONE MASTER

**Name:** SCD ONE  
**Long name:** SCD ONE — ColicoDerviese Social Super App  
**Tagline:** Tutto il Club. Una sola app.

## Mission
Become the memorable, high-scale digital place where an unlimited number of people can live and transact with SCD: sport, social, community, services, events and verified club activity.

## Core experiences
Pulse, SCD Week, Calendar, Teams, Matchday, Social/Community, Media/Newsroom, Twin, Family/Athlete context, Join, events, tournaments entry, field requests, tickets, cards and public services.

## Product standard
Fast, mobile-first, adaptive, social, modern, highly responsive, real-data-first and transaction-ready.

## Boundary
Internal association management belongs to SCD CORE.
Deep sponsor/CRM development belongs to SCD GROW.
Command/orchestration belongs to shared R22.

## Command
`SCDONE:RUN` invokes `WEBAPP:MASTER`.
